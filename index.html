Holiday Menu Planner: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Holiday Menu Planner does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information.

What is stored

The menus you create, your settings and any backups are saved only in your browser's local extension storage on your device. We cannot see them. They are removed if you uninstall the extension.

What we do not do

The extension has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not send your menus or any other data to us or to anyone else.

Recipe links

If you click "Find recipe", the extension opens a web search for that dish name in a new browser tab. Once that page opens, the search engine's own privacy policy applies.

Exports

PDF, Word and backup files are created on your device and saved wherever you choose.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: help@amproductions.site
